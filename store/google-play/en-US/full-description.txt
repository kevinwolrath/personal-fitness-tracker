Build a fitness routine around your day with Body Anvil.

YOUR DAILY PLAN
See your planned activities, meals and workouts in one daily log. Mark activities done, record measurements and return to earlier days to make corrections.

WORKOUTS AND EXERCISE GUIDES
Follow your planned strength session, log a different dose and open illustrated exercise guides. Explore muscle coverage and record soreness or injury to help inform your training choices.

PROGRESS FROM YOUR RECORDS
View weight, waist, workouts and other available measurements over time. Charts use recorded information and distinguish calculated values and estimates from observations.

BRING YOUR INFORMATION TOGETHER
On compatible Android devices, optionally connect Health Connect to read supported health and fitness records. Permissions and available data determine what appears. Optionally keep a private copy of your records on the Body Anvil server and create expiring read-only links to share them.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose Hero Forge or Classic appearance, choose energy display units and configure your plan through setup answers and the supported AI-assisted configuration workflow. AI-assisted configuration uses an external AI service of your choice; it is not an automatic coaching service.

Body Anvil is a personal planning and tracking tool. Exercise guides and muscle coverage are informational and do not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
