Build a fitness routine around your day with Body Anvil.

ONE CONTINUOUS DAILY LOG
See your planned activity, record what you did and return to earlier days. Keep workouts, meals and measurements together instead of switching between separate trackers.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN
Follow strength sessions, mark exercises complete and open illustrated exercise guides. Choose a shorter session when time is limited. Review muscle coverage and record soreness or injuries to help organise your training.

MAKE THE PLAN YOURS
Use the first-run setup to describe your goals and equipment. Configure with AI lets you copy instructions to an AI assistant, then review and apply a plan update in the app.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Record measurements and review your history. On supported Android devices, you can choose to read supported activity and measurement data from Health Connect. Access is optional and requires your permission.

YOUR HISTORY, ON YOUR DEVICE
The diary uses local storage. Export your history as JSON for your own backup and restore it later. Screenshot import is optional: it requires your own Anthropic API key and sends only the images you select when you choose to read them. Review the extracted values before saving.

Choose the Hero Forge appearance or Classic light in Settings. Optional reminders help you keep track of your plan.
